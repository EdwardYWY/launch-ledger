Methodology

Every estimate uses the same sealed snapshot. Coverage, assumptions and the size of the scenario range are visible for each candidate.

Selection

Valuation

TVL × peer multiple × age factor × momentum factor. The peer multiple blends the sector median market-cap-to-TVL ratio with the global median using geometric weights. Sector weight is n/(n+8), and individual ratios are clipped to 0.05–8×.

Time and momentum

The age factor rises from 0.75 to 1.15 with days tracked, using an exponential curve with a 270-day scale. Missing age uses 0.85. The momentum factor exponentiates weekly TVL change clipped at ±25%, with a neutral multiplier when the field is missing.

Scenario range

The central estimate is divided and multiplied by a 1.5–2.2× band. Missing inputs, fewer than five sector peers or a review flag widen it. The range shows uncertainty in the valuation assumptions. Listing dates measure tracking history, and token metadata can be incomplete.
